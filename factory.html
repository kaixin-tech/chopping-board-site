From Rice Husk to Finished Board

Raw rice husk is cleaned and milled, then blended with a food-grade starch binder as the natural binding agent. The mixture is moulded under high temperature and pressure into a dense board — with no glue and no harmful chemical additives. Colour, logo and juice groove are built into the process rather than added afterwards.

  • ✓In-house moulding presses give us control over lead time and consistency
  • ✓Dense, consistent surface from batch to batch
  • ✓Colour matched under controlled light and checked on reorder
